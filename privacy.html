Privacy Policy

This website (https://clickboxpk.github.io/drivingtest/) is the PSDC NRK driving theory test practice portal run by PAFWA Skills Development Centre.

What we collect

The student name and father name you type before a test are used only inside your browser to print your result sheet. They are not sent to or stored on our servers. If you use the answer-editing tool (admin only), settings are saved in your own browser storage.

Advertising

This site shows banner advertisements served by third-party advertising partners (such as Adsterra). These partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads and measure performance. Ads are never shown during a test paper. You can limit ad personalisation or block cookies from your browser settings.

Share cards

The result card image is created on your own device. It is shared only when you choose to download or share it.
